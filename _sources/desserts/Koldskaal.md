# Koldskål
A classic danish dessert served usually during the summer

## Ingredients
-[] 1 L Buttermilk
-[] 2 Eggs
-[] 2 tsbp Sugar
-[] 1/2 Vanilla pod
-[] 1 Lemon

## Instructions
1. Seperate the egg yolk from the egg whites. Discard or save the egg whites for your own purposes
2. Cut open and scrabe the vanilla bean pod (get all the tiny seeds) and add them to the sugar and egg yolks. Whisk it all together until frothy and light.
3. Add the buttermilk and combine, grate the lemon zest and squeeze about half a lemon. Combine everything and adjust with either sugar or lemon to taste.

## Serving
Koldskål is best enjoyed during the summer, in a bowl and with kammerjunker, strawberries or other biscuits/fruits/berries added on top. Enjoy:).
