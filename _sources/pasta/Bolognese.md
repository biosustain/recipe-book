# Pasta Bolognese

## Ingredients
- 200 g tagliatele
- 1 can tomato sauce
- 500 g minced meat
- 1 garlic clove
- 1 onion
- Paprica powder
- salt and black pepper to taste
- Shredded cheese

## Instructions
1. Cook the pasta in salted boiling water according to the package directions. Drain.
2. Saute the onion and minced meat
3. Add tomato sauce, garlic and spices
4. Combine the pasta and minced meat and add cheese
