# Fresh Spring rolls recipe

## Ingredients

- Rice paper
- cooked meat (optional)
- sliced cucumber sticks
- sliced carrot sticks
- vermicelli noodles
- lettuce
- herbs
- fish sauce


## Instructions

1. Briefly soak rice paper in water and lay it out flat on a plate
2. Add a small leaf of lettuce on the soaked rice paper
3. Add cucumber and/or carrot sticks on top
4. Add vermicelli noodles next to the sticks
5. Top with herbs and meat (optional)
6. Roll the rice papers
