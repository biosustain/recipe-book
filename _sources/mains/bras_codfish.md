# Title

## Ingredients

(For 4 people)

- 1 Onions
- 2 Garlic
- Codfish
- 2 eggs
- Parsley
- Olives
- 1 Bay leaf

## Instructions

1. Peel the potatoes and cut them into very thin strips (matchsticks). Soak them in water for about half an hour to remove excess starch. Alternatively, you can skip this step and use store-bought shoestring potatoes.

2. Meanwhile, remove the skin and bones from the cod and shred the fish by hand.

3. Drain the potatoes and pat them thoroughly dry with a kitchen towel or paper towels. Fry them in preheated oil.

4. In the meantime, heat olive oil in a frying pan and sauté the onion (peeled and sliced ​​into half-moons), the garlic (peeled and minced), and the bay leaf.

5. Once the onion and garlic begin to soften, add the shredded cod and cover the pan.

6. Cook over low heat until the cod turns white.

7. Remove the bay leaf from the mixture and add the fried potatoes.

8. Add the beaten eggs and stir gently, ensuring they coat all the other ingredients. Do not let the eggs cook for too long or become too dry.

9. Before serving, sprinkle with chopped parsley and garnish with the olives.
