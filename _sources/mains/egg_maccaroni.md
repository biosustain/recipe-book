# Egg Maccaroni

A dish loved by kids, me included @enryh-testing. Fried
pasta with eggs.

## Ingredients

- 500gr of pasta
- 4 eggs
- cooking oil (sunflower or rape seed oil)
- optional: some tofu or sausage

## Instructions

- **cook** the pasta rather al-dente
- optional: cut tofu or sausage into cubes
- heat pan with cooking oil
- fry pasta a bit along the optional ingrediants
- whip the eggs and add briefly to the pasta and mix
