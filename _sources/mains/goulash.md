# Traditional Goulash Recipe
# Hungarian goulash

## Ingredients

(For 4 People)

- Beef meat
- onion
- paprika
- water
- salt

## Instructions


1. **Sauté the base:** Cook finely chopped onions in fat, remove from the heat, and stir in plenty of sweet paprika.
2. **Brown the beef:** Add diced beef, season with salt and caraway seeds, and cook until the meat is browned all over.
3. **Simmer the meat:** Pour in a little water, cover, and simmer over low heat until the meat is almost tender.
4. **Add vegetables:** Stir in diced potatoes, carrots, and root vegetables, then pour in plenty of water to make a soup.
5. **Finish with noodles:** Simmer until the vegetables are tender, adding pinched noodles (*csipetke*) during the last few minutes of cooking.
